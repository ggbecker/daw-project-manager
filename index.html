Everything Your Library Needs

A complete toolkit for managing your music production projects

⚡

Smart Scanning

Two-phase scanning: instant detection of DAW type and file path, then optional deep extraction of BPM, key, and version on demand. No waiting around for large libraries.

🎛️

Rich Project Metadata

Edit display names, BPM, musical key, phase, notes, and tags per project. Change the production phase right from the table with a dropdown — no need to open each project.

✅

Task Queue

See all unfinished todos across every project in one unified view. Grouped by project and sorted by pending count. Check off tasks without ever leaving the queue.

🎵

Preview Player

Play preview songs directly in the app with full seek controls. Arrow keys = ±5s, Ctrl+arrow = ±30s. Play and review your work without switching windows.

📦

Release Management

Group projects into releases with artwork, description, and release date. Attach stems, mixes, and other files. Navigate your catalogue with a sortable table view.

⌨️

Keyboard-First

Full keyboard navigation in the project table. P preview, O open in DAW, D details, F open folder. Cmd/Alt+← to go back. Everything reachable without a mouse.

🔍

Fuzzy Search

Search works the way you think. Type scattered characters and still find what you're looking for. Consistent fuzzy matching across projects, releases, tasks, and playlists.

📊

Statistics

Charts and summaries of your entire library — production phases, DAW breakdown, activity over time. See the big picture of your creative output at a glance.

👤

Multiple Profiles

Switch between producer profiles instantly. Each profile has its own scan roots, projects, and settings — perfect for studios, collaborators, or separating personal and client work.

☁️

Google Drive Sync

Back up and restore your project database to Google Drive. Keep your metadata safe and accessible across machines without syncing raw project files. Optional auto-backup runs on a schedule — every 30 minutes, hourly, every 6 hours, or daily.

🎚️

Customizable Phases

Rename, reorder, and color your own production phases instead of a fixed pipeline. Mark any phase as a "finished" state, and every change is logged to the project timeline.

🧩

Project & Todo Templates

Save any existing project as a reusable template — BPM, key, DAW, and starter files included — and spin up new projects pre-filled from it. Pair with reusable todo checklists for repeatable workflows.

🎼

Music Player

Browse and play preview songs across your whole library, not just one project at a time — a full queue-based player on desktop with a Camelot-wheel key-compatible auto-queue, and a "Now Playing" screen on mobile.

🎧

Playlists Mobile

On Android and iOS, create ordered playlists from your preview songs and listen to your library in sequence — great for reviewing work on the go.

🖥️

Background & Tray Mode Desktop

Close the window and the app keeps running — a tray icon on Windows or a menu-bar icon on macOS gives you one-click backups and work-session controls without keeping the main window open.

📤

Smart Sharing

Share a preview song straight to the OS share sheet. WAV, AIFF, and FLAC files are automatically transcoded — to AAC on macOS, to MP3 on Windows via a bundled ffmpeg — so they open in any app on the other end.

🌍

9 Languages

Fully localized in English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, Italian, German, Russian, Japanese, and Chinese. Switch language any time from the app settings.
